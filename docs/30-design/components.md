# Components

*Every component a screen may use. Screens import them from
`@/components/ui` (shadcn's, restyled through [tokens](tokens.md)) and
`@/components/lifeos` (LifeOS's own, built from shadcn's). Nothing else.*

How shadcn components are customised:

- **Through the theme first.** Most of the look comes from shadcn's theme
  variables in the theme file.
- **Then in the component's own file**, in its variant definitions (`cva`),
  once, for everyone. The variants listed here are the only ones.
- **Never on a screen.** A screen may place a component (margin, grid
  position, width in a layout) but never restyles it: no colour, size,
  radius, shadow or font classes.

A component not listed here is added to this page before it is used.

## shadcn components

| Component | Variants and sizes | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Button** | `primary` (ink, or the Type colour inside `TypeScope`), `secondary`, `outline` (card fill with the hairline edge, no border), `ghost`, `ghost-destructive` (red text, no fill), `destructive`, `link` (the Type's text colour, underlined on hover, no height or padding of its own). | **Badge** | `secondary`, `outline` (muted fill, muted text), `destructive` (tinted), plus `TypeBadge`. 22px pill, 12px, weight 600. | Short labels only: a Label, a state, a count. Not a button; a value chosen in a form may end in an X that removes it (`onRemove`, 16px, with its label). |
| **Input**, **Textarea** | Filled with `--input`, no border; on focus the card fill with the 3px halo. Default 40px; `sm` 32px in tables and toolbars; `lg` 52px with 16px text, capture's line. | Every input has a visible label, except search, which has a search icon. |
| **Select** | As Input, with a chevron. | Options open in a menu styled as DropdownMenu. |

| **Checkbox** | 18px, 6px radius, 1.5px `--input-strong` edge; checked fills with `--primary`. With a `colour`, the edge and the fill are that Type's colour. | **Toggle** | Icon only, 32px; pressed fills the icon (the Type colour inside `TypeScope`). `lg`: 40px with a 20px icon. | Star a Task, pin a Note; `lg`, heart a Recipe (on its card and its page; Joe, 20 October). |

| **Separator** | 1px, foreground at 7%. | Rare: between a dialog's body and footer, and between the FilterPanel's parts. Space separates everything else. |
| **DropdownMenu** | `--popover`, 18px radius, `--shadow-pop`, 6px padding; 36px items, 8px radius, highlighted with `--accent`; 12px muted group labels; shortcuts in Kbd on the right. | Actions on an Item ("Convert to"). | **Popover** | As DropdownMenu, 14px padding for forms. | A small form tied to a control (planning a Recipe). |
| **Tooltip** | Ink fill, 12px weight 600, 8px radius. | Names an icon-only control, and its shortcut. |
| **Dialog** | `--popover`, 24px radius, `--shadow-dialog`, over `--overlay`; centred, never attached to an edge (inset 18px on a phone), as tall as its content up to the screen (36px short of it), then scrolling inside, its body in a ScrollArea where it has one (ItemDialog). | **Sonner** (toast) | `--popover`, 18px radius, `--shadow-pop`, bottom right (bottom on a phone, above where the Fab sits). | Confirming what just happened, with Undo where possible. |
| **Accordion** | Bold trigger with a tile and a chevron; no lines. `sm` trigger: 14px, 36px high, no tile. | Groups that can collapse (exercises); `sm`, the FilterPanel's collapsed filters. |
| **Avatar** | 30px circle, `--secondary`, initials 11px weight 700. | People. |
| **Kbd** | Geist Mono 10.5px, 18px high, 6px radius, `--muted`. | Shortcuts in buttons, menus and tooltips. |
| **Alert** | Card fill with icon, bold title and muted description; optionally an action on the right (`AlertAction`: one or two small buttons, stacked under the text on a phone). | A state worth noticing on the page (offline; notifications off, with Turn on). Not for decoration. |
| **Command** | In a Dialog near the top (560px; `CommandDialog`), on a phone too, closed by a tap outside it. A 48px input with a search icon, bare over a line, then results grouped by Type under 12px muted labels, each a 52px item with a small TypeTile, the title (the match in bold) and a muted 12px line; the highlighted item `--accent`, moved with the arrow keys and chosen with Enter. | **ScrollArea** | Wherever content scrolls: the page, the drawer, a Dialog's body, a long menu. A 6px `--scroll-thumb` pill 2px from the edge, shown while scrolling or hovered; no track. Sideways too (Tabs). | Every scrolling box. No browser scrollbars anywhere: what still scrolls natively (a long text field) gets a thin scrollbar in `--scroll-thumb`. |
