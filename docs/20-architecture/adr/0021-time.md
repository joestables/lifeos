# ADR-0021: Time

**Status:** Provisionally agreed · 2026-10-09

**Amended:** 2026-10-18 by Joe's comments on the Stage 4 specs (three slots: no snack).

## Context

v1's Planner calculated virtual occurrences on the fly, with the engine
duplicated in TypeScript and SQL; virtual occurrences could not hold links.

## Decision

- The core table carries a small standard set of time fields used by Plan:
  `starts_at`, `ends_at`, `all_day`, `due_on`, **`do_on`** (+ optional
  `do_at`), `slot`.
- **Tasks have a do-on date as well as a due date.** Due drives "overdue" in
  Inbox; do-on is when you intend to act. A Task with only a deadline is
  shown on Plan on its due date.
- **Meals use fixed slots:** breakfast, lunch, dinner (Joe, 18 October).
- The standard fields hold the **plan**; a Type's detail table holds what
  actually happened (e.g. a workout's actual start and duration), enabling
  planned-vs-done.
- **Repeating items are generated occurrences:** a series rule creates real
  Items for a rolling window (≈8–12 weeks), each carrying the series' id.
  Rejected: virtual occurrences calculated on the fly.
- Moments are stored in UTC; all-day items and meal dates as plain dates;
  **one home timezone per person** (Europe/London) rather than a hard-coded
  clock.

## Consequences

Every occurrence can be linked, ticked, shared and measured. How series
behave: [ADR-0022](0022-repeating-items.md) (Provisionally agreed).
